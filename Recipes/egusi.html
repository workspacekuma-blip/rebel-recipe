Egusi Recipe

Description

Egusi soup is a popular Nigerian soup made from egusi seeds, which are ground into a paste and used as a thickener. It is typically cooked with vegetables and meat or fish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup egusi seeds
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Soak the egusi seeds in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Grind the soaked egusi seeds into a fine paste.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and sauté the chopped onion and minced garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add the egusi paste and stir well to combine.
  5. Gradually add the water while stirring to prevent lumps.
  6. Simmer for 20-25 minutes or until the soup is thick and well-seasoned.
